Online Therapy in MA, CT, ME, NH, VT & DC

Online counseling and therapy for life transitions, stress, anxiety, grief, and the moments when you feel stuck.

Support that fits into your life

Life can get busy, especially when you’re working, caring for others, or navigating a period of change. Online therapy offers a way to make space for yourself without adding a commute to your week.

I work with people who are used to carrying a lot: living up to expectations, holding things together, and being the one others rely on. When life changes, the ways you’ve always navigated life may no longer fit.

Together, we can make sense of what you’re experiencing, understand what’s keeping you stuck, and figure out what comes next. I combine clinical support with practical tools to help you:

  • Make sense of your emotions: Understand what you’re experiencing rather than ignoring, avoiding, or fighting it.
  • Reconnect with what is important: Clarify your needs and values, beyond what is expected of you.
  • Strengthen your well-being: Develop practical skills to navigate stress, anxiety, depression, and burnout.
  • Take meaningful steps forward: Recognize patterns that leave you feeling stuck and develop strategies to move toward what matters.

Online Therapy Across Six Licensed Jurisdictions

I offer online therapy to adults who are physically located in Massachusetts, Connecticut, Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, or Washington, DC at the time of their session.

Whether you are navigating a life transition, feeling overwhelmed or stuck, or looking for support with anxiety, stress, grief, burnout, or other concerns, online therapy can provide a flexible way to connect from a private space that works for you.

If you are located in Massachusetts and prefer in-person care, I also offer sessions in Harvard Square, Boston, Cambridge, Brookline, and surrounding communities. Explore in-person therapy.

Where You Are Located Matters

Because I am a licensed clinical social worker, I am required to follow the laws and regulations of the state or jurisdiction where you are physically located when we meet.

I currently offer telehealth to clients who are physically located in Massachusetts, Connecticut, Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, and Washington, DC, consistent with my professional licensure.

If you anticipate traveling or being in another state during our work together, please let me know. We can discuss whether I am able to provide care while you are there.

What to Expect

I’m Alice Kim, LICSW, an Asian American psychotherapist with more than 25 years of clinical experience. My approach combines thoughtful clinical support with practical tools, meeting you where you are.

Online sessions provide a private space to slow down, reflect, and focus on what you need without having to travel to an office.

My practice is private pay and out of network at $250 per 50-minute session. I offer a free 15-minute consultation so we can talk about what you’re looking for, where you’ll be located during sessions, and whether we might be a good fit.

You don’t need to have everything figured out before you reach out. We can start with where you are.
