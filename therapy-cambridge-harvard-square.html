A Space to Slow Down, Reflect, and Connect

Sometimes getting out of your usual environment can make it easier to pause and focus on yourself.

I offer in-person counseling and therapy for adults navigating life transitions, stress, anxiety, burnout, grief, relationship and family changes, and periods when you feel stuck or uncertain about what comes next.

I work with people who are used to carrying a lot: living up to expectations, holding things together, and being the one others rely on. Therapy can offer a space where you don't have to be the one holding everything together.

Walk-and-Talk Therapy

Walk-and-talk therapy combines traditional talk therapy with movement and time outdoors. Rather than meeting in an office, we meet while walking in an outdoor setting. For some people, being outside and moving their body can make it easier to reflect, process emotions, and have a more natural conversation.

Walk-and-talk therapy can be a good fit for many people, but it is not appropriate for every situation or clinical need. We can discuss whether this approach makes sense for you based on your goals, preferences, and circumstances.

Learn more about walk-and-talk therapy in the FAQ.

What We Can Work On Together

A transition isn't necessarily the event itself. It's what happens internally when the old way of living no longer fits.

You may be adjusting to a new stage of life, questioning your career, navigating changing family roles, experiencing burnout, or simply wondering what you want next.

Together, we can:

  • Make sense of your emotions: Understand what you're experiencing rather than ignoring, avoiding, or fighting it.
  • Reconnect with what is important: Clarify your needs and values, beyond what is expected of you.
  • Strengthen your well-being: Develop practical skills to navigate stress, anxiety, depression, and burnout.
  • Take meaningful steps forward: Recognize patterns that leave you feeling stuck and develop strategies to move toward what matters.

An Approach That Meets You Where You Are
