Walk-and-Talk Counseling and Therapy in the Greater Boston Area

The Same Thoughtful Work, a Different Setting

Step outside. Connect with yourself and the world around you.

Sessions don’t always have to take place in an office. Walk-and-talk therapy brings our conversations outdoors, combining psychotherapy with gentle movement and time in nature. For some people, being outside and moving their body can make it easier to reflect, process emotions, and have a more natural conversation.

Walk-and-talk therapy is not simply “talking while walking.” It is still therapy, with the same focus on your goals, emotional well-being, and the therapeutic relationship. The difference is that the setting and movement may offer a different experience than sitting across from a therapist in an office.

Walk-and-talk sessions may be particularly helpful for people who find traditional office-based therapy too formal or prefer a more active and experiential approach. It is also an option for clients who feel more comfortable talking side-by-side rather than face-to-face.

Where do walk-and-talk sessions take place?

Walk-and-talk sessions take place outdoors in a mutually agreed-upon public location in the Greater Boston area, such as Cambridge, Brookline, Newton, Lexington, and Wellesley.

Because these sessions take place outdoors, privacy cannot be guaranteed in the same way as in an office. We will talk about strategies for maintaining your privacy and comfort while walking.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Walk-and-talk sessions are weather dependent. If conditions make it unsafe or uncomfortable to meet outside, we can reschedule or transition to a telehealth or in-office session.

Is walk-and-talk therapy right for everyone?

Walk-and-talk therapy can be a good fit for many people, but it is not appropriate for every situation or clinical need. We can discuss whether this approach makes sense for you based on your goals, preferences, and circumstances.
