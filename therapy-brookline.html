In-Person Counseling and Therapy in Brookline

A space for yourself. Time to pause, breathe, and slow down.

When your days are filled with responsibilities, it can be difficult to step back and notice how you’re doing. Meeting in person offers time to take a breath, step out of your usual routines, and give attention to what you’re experiencing.

I offer in-person counseling and therapy for life transitions, stress, anxiety, grief, and the moments when you feel stuck. You may be facing a specific challenge or simply noticing that the way you’re living no longer reflects what matters to you.

You don’t have to wait until things feel unmanageable to seek support.

Making Room for What Matters to You

Counseling and therapy can be a place to explore the difference between what others need from you, your ambitions, and what you need for yourself. There is room for uncertainty, complicated feelings, and questions you haven’t yet found words for.

I’m Alice Kim, LICSW, an Asian American psychotherapist with more than 25 years of clinical experience. My approach combines thoughtful exploration with practical tools, helping you understand yourself more deeply, navigate challenges, and take meaningful steps toward what matters to you.

Meeting in Brookline

My office is located at 1101 Beacon Street, Brookline, near Boston University’s Charles River campus and the Longwood Medical Area. Sessions are by appointment only.

The office is near the Hawes Street stop on the MBTA Green Line C branch.

Driving: metered parking on Beacon Street; some side streets are two-hour parking. Please check street signs.

I welcome adults who live, work, or study in Brookline and nearby Boston neighborhoods, including professionals in healthcare, academia, finance, and other fields navigating the demands of work and personal life.

View the Brookline office on a map.

Getting Started

My practice is private pay and out-of-network, with a fee of $250 per 50-minute session.

A free 15-minute consultation offers an opportunity to talk about what you’re looking for, ask questions, and consider whether working together feels like a good fit.

I also offer in-person appointments in Harvard Square, Cambridge and walk-and-talk sessions. Prefer to meet online? Explore online therapy.
