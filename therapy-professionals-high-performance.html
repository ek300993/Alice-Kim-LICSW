Counseling and Therapy for Professionals in Demanding Careers and High-Performance Environments

You worked hard to get here. How does it feel to be here?

You may be hardworking, driven, and ambitious. Others rely on you to function at a high level, solve problems, and keep going. Yet you may find it difficult to slow down or switch off, feel less connected to work that once mattered, or wonder why the success you worked toward no longer feels satisfying.

Or perhaps a change in your health, shifting family responsibilities, or a new stage of life has prompted you to reconsider what success means to you — and what you need and want now.

Space Beyond What You Accomplish

This work is not about maximizing your productivity. Your hard work, ambition and drive has gotten you where you are. It is about understanding how you’re doing, what your responsibilities and expectations ask of you, and what you want for your life alongside your commitments.

How We Can Work Together

Over my 25+ years as a therapist — working across hospitals, academia, research universities, startups, tech companies, and corporate environments — I have spent a lot of time sitting with people who are used to carrying heavy loads.

Because I have worked both inside these high-pressure cultures and alongside clients navigating them, I get the unique demands they place on your mental health. Here is what that means for our work together:

  • I speak your language: You don’t have to waste energy explaining organizational culture, the relentless pace of high-stakes environments, or the heavy weight of expectations. I understand these dynamics, allowing us to dive right in. Together, we will get curious about your specific situation and culture, addressing what you are actually experiencing: burnout, imposter syndrome, perfectionism, and the constant struggle to turn off work and disconnect.
  • We look at the whole picture: I help you understand how your environment and high standards are affecting your mental health, moving past everyday stress to untangle the roots of your burnout or “stuckness.”
  • Practical change: We make sense of those pressures together and move toward clarity and personal growth that is relevant, realistic, and sustainable. We focus on what matters most: helping you reconnect with your values, your well-being, and your next purposeful step.

Getting Started

I offer in-office sessions in Harvard Square and Brookline, walk-and-talk therapy in the Greater Boston area, and online therapy.

A free 15-minute consultation is an opportunity to talk about what brings you here, ask questions, and see whether we might be a good fit.
