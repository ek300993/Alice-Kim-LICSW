Learn More About My Approach

Life moves fast, and sometimes the maps we used to navigate no longer work.

A transition isn’t necessarily the event itself. It’s what happens internally when the old way of living no longer fits.

You may find yourself questioning who you are, what matters, and where you’re headed. You may know that something needs to change without knowing exactly what comes next.

Transitions can bring a complicated mix of emotions—hope and excitement one day, uncertainty and overwhelm the next. Even positive changes can leave us feeling unsettled as we adjust to a new sense of self, new expectations, or an unfamiliar path forward.

I can help you navigate:

  • Lifestage and Identity Transitions: Milestones like young adulthood, parenthood, midlife, or older adulthood naturally prompt a reassessment of who you are. “What is it I want at this season of my life? What is meaningful and purposeful to me now when my primary roles have shifted?”
  • Relationship and Family Transitions: Shifts in intimacy or evolving family roles are creating relational challenges. “Can I navigate this change without losing myself in the process? How do I prioritize my own needs and still stay connected to the relationships that are meaningful to me?”
  • Professional Transitions: Burnout and stress leads to questioning if your work still aligns with your values. “How do I choose the next step when the path ahead feels uncertain? How do I know if I’m making the right decisions?”

You don’t have to figure it out on your own. With the support of a professional, a transition can become an opportunity to understand yourself more deeply and create a life that feels more intentional and aligned with who you are now.

My Services

How We Can Work Together

Life Transition Counseling

You may be doing everything you’re supposed to do and still feel uncertain about what’s next. Whether you are struggling to adjust to a major shift or simply feeling the need for growth and clarity, you don’t have to navigate this space alone.

Transitions can be unsettling. They tend to throw off our daily routines, shake our confidence, and leave us feeling stuck and exhausted. My practice is a steady space where we can process these changes together through:

  • Support for the Struggle: If a recent change has left you feeling overwhelmed, anxious, or unable to function as you normally would, we can work to stabilize your mood, manage your stress, and help you find your footing again.
  • Support for the Shift: If you are in a period of change and feel that you want to live more intentionally, deepen your self-awareness, and align your daily life with your core values, we can use this time of transition as a catalyst for meaningful growth.

Therapy

Life can be complex, and sometimes challenges feel persistent. You may notice you are not feeling like yourself, or sense that your usual ways of coping are no longer helping. If you are feeling overwhelmed, emotionally stuck, or weighed down by recurring patterns, therapy provides a compassionate, nonjudgmental space to explore these experiences with curiosity and care.

Together, we will uncover what is beneath the surface, reduce symptoms that hold you back, and develop more effective ways to manage your emotions, thoughts, and behaviors. This is a safe, supportive space to navigate life's complexities, including:

  • Everyday Stress & Burnout: Career pressure, perfectionism, and caregiver fatigue.
  • Relationships & Family: Couples dynamics, parenting challenges, and boundary-setting.
  • Older Adults & Aging: Navigating later-life transitions, existential realities, and the emotional impact of mortality.
  • Grief & Loss: Navigating bereavement, life changes, and chronic health issues.
  • Mental Health Conditions: Anxiety, depression, panic, ADHD and executive functioning, and processing trauma.
