How Can I Help?

25+ Years of Clinical Experience Asian American Psychotherapist, LICSW Adjunct Professor at Boston College Transitional Intelligence (TQ) Certified

I work with adults who care about their relationships, work, and commitments. You may be used to meeting expectations, holding things together, and being someone others rely on—yet find it hard to slow down and notice what you need and want.

Our work offers time to process what you are experiencing, and explore ways to make room for yourself within the life you care about. Together, we can:

  • Make sense of your emotions: Understand what you’re feeling rather than ignoring, avoiding, or fighting it.
  • Reconnect with what is important: Clarify your needs and values beyond what is expected of you.
  • Strengthen your well-being: Develop practical skills to navigate stress, care for yourself, and communicate your needs and boundaries.
  • Take meaningful steps forward: Recognize patterns that leave you feeling stuck and develop strategies to move toward what matters.

Life Transition Counseling: Find Your Way Through Change

Some changes are chosen; others arrive unexpectedly. Sometimes, there is no single event—just a growing question about whether the way you’re living still reflects what is important to you.

Some transitions we can work through include:

Life Milestones & Identity Shifts

“What is meaningful and purposeful to me in this season of my life?”

Relationship & Family Transitions

“How can I honor my needs while staying connected to the people I care about?”

Professional Transitions

“Is my professional identity still aligned with what I actually want?”

You don’t have to figure it all out at once.

Therapy for Mental Health & Life Challenges

Life is complex, and sometimes life transitions and mental health intersect. Therapy offers a space to understand what’s beneath the surface, develop more effective ways of coping, and make meaningful changes.

Support for:

  • Anxiety & depression
  • Stress & burnout
  • ADHD & executive functioning
  • Grief & loss
  • Relationships, family & boundaries
  • Caregiving stress
  • Life changes & aging
