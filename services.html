Life Transition Counseling & Evidence-Based Therapy

Life Transitions Counseling

Helping people navigate the moments when life no longer feels quite like it used to.

  • Young Adulthood & Independence: Stepping into full adulthood and navigating new levels of independence is a significant adjustment. I help you manage the uncertainty of “finding your way” while building a stable foundation for your future.
  • Mid-Life Realignment: Mid-life often feels like a paradox: you have built the life you were “supposed” to want, yet you feel restless or disconnected. I help you peel back the layers of outdated expectations to ensure your future is built on who you are today.
  • Older Adulthood & Vitality: Aging brings new perspectives and unique challenges. I provide a space to help you find purpose, maintain overall well-being, and navigate the changes that come with this stage of life.

  • Relocation & Geographic Shifts: Moving to a new city means losing your routine and support system. I help you process the impact of these changes and offer guidance in rebuilding a sense of belonging in your new environment.
  • Career Pivot & Burnout Recovery: Burnout can signal that your work no longer matches your values. I help you understand the root of your burnout and manage the anxiety that comes with adjusting your career path.
  • Executive & Leadership Transitions: Changing roles or taking on organizational responsibilities can be stressful. I provide support for you to process the pressures and uncertainty of high-stakes career changes.
  • Retirement Readiness: Retirement is more than a financial change—it is a major shift in your identity, time, and daily energy. I help you navigate this “in-between” stage so you can design a meaningful, healthy, and fulfilling next chapter.

  • Partnership & Parenting Adjustments: Committing to a partner or starting a family changes how we see ourselves and use our time. I help you manage these transitions so you can maintain your sense of self while deepening your connections.
  • Breakup & Separation Recovery: Ending a partnership is a major transition that carries deep emotional complexity. I provide a supportive space for you to heal from the emotional impact and rebuild your identity and life.
  • Empty Nest & New Beginnings: As children grow and leave home, your role as a parent shifts. I help you process this loss and rediscover who you are and how to relate in this next chapter of life.
  • Adult Children & Family Dynamics: If you have grown but your family’s old patterns have not, it can cause constant tension. I help you set clear boundaries and improve communication, even when family dynamics feel stuck.
  • Caregiving for Loved Ones: Supporting family members with a chronic health condition or caring for aging parents is a difficult and often lonely transition. I help you navigate the complex grief, chronic stress, and identity shifts so you can protect your well-being while caring for others.

Therapy Services

We all experience seasons where we feel overwhelmed, uncertain, or in need of space to process what’s unfolding. I offer therapy for individuals who want to focus on healing, adjustment, or emotional growth.

This is a supportive space to address:

  • Everyday Stress & Burnout: Career pressure, perfectionism, and caregiver fatigue.
  • Relationships & Family: Couples dynamics, parenting challenges, and boundary-setting.
  • Older Adults & Aging: Navigating later-life transitions, existential realities, and the emotional impact of mortality.
  • Grief & Loss: Navigating bereavement, life changes, and chronic health issues.
  • Mental Health Conditions: Anxiety, depression, panic, ADHD and executive functioning, and processing trauma.
