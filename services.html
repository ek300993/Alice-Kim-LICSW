Life Transitions Counseling

Helping people navigate the moments when life no longer feels quite like it used to.

Young Adulthood & Independence

Together, we’ll manage the uncertainty of “finding your way” while building a stable foundation for your future.

Mid-Life Realignment

We’ll peel back the layers of outdated expectations and build your future around who you are today.

Older Adulthood & Vitality

We’ll explore what brings you purpose, support your overall well-being, and navigate the changes that come with this stage of life.

Relocation & Geographic Shifts

We’ll process the impact of these changes and work on rebuilding a sense of belonging in your new environment.

Career Pivot & Burnout Recovery

We’ll explore the root of your burnout and manage the anxiety that comes with adjusting your career path.

Executive & Leadership Transitions

We’ll process the pressures and uncertainty of high-stakes career changes.

Retirement Readiness

We’ll navigate this “in-between” stage so you can design a meaningful, healthy, and fulfilling next chapter.

Partnership & Parenting Adjustments

We’ll work through these transitions so you can maintain your sense of self while deepening your connections.

Breakup & Separation Recovery

We’ll create a supportive space for you to heal from the emotional impact and rebuild your identity and life.

Empty Nest & New Beginnings

We’ll process this loss and explore who you are and how to relate in this next chapter of life.

Adult Children & Family Dynamics

We’ll work on setting clear boundaries and improving communication, even when family dynamics feel stuck.

Caregiving for Loved Ones

We’ll navigate the complex grief, chronic stress, and identity shifts so you can protect your well-being while caring for others.

Therapy Services

We all experience seasons where we feel overwhelmed, uncertain, or in need of space to process what’s unfolding. Together, we’ll focus on healing, adjustment, or emotional growth.

Learn more about therapy for anxiety, stress and burnout.

This is a supportive space to address:

  • Everyday Stress & Burnout: Career pressure, perfectionism, and caregiver fatigue.
  • Relationships & Family: Couples dynamics, parenting challenges, and boundary-setting.
  • Older Adults & Aging: Navigating later-life transitions, existential realities, and the emotional impact of mortality.
  • Grief & Loss: Navigating bereavement, life changes, and chronic health issues.
  • Mental Health Conditions: Anxiety, depression, panic, ADHD and executive functioning, and processing trauma.
