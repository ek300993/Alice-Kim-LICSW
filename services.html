Life Transitions Counseling

Clinical Focus Areas

Professional Transitions

  • Career Pivot & Burnout Recovery: Burnout often signals that your work no longer matches your values. I help you understand the root of your burnout and manage the anxiety that comes with adjusting your career path.
  • Executive & High-Performer Transitions: Changing roles or taking on new leadership can be stressful. I provide clinical expertise for high performers and a private space for you to process the pressure and uncertainty of high-stakes career changes.
  • Retirement Readiness: Retirement is more than a financial change—it is a shift in your identity. I help you navigate the “in-between” stage so you can find a meaningful, generative, and healthy next chapter.

Relationship & Family Transitions

  • Partnership & Parenting Adjustments: Committing to a partner or starting a family changes how we see ourselves and our time. I help you manage these transitions so you can maintain your sense of self while deepening your connections.
  • Breakup & Separation Recovery: Ending a partnership is a major transition that carries deep emotional complexity. I provide a supportive space for you to heal from the emotional impact and rebuild your identity.
  • Empty Nest & New Beginnings: As children grow and leave home, your role as a parent shifts. I help you process this loss and rediscover who you are in this next chapter of life.
  • Caregiving for Loved Ones: Supporting an aging or ill family member is a difficult and often lonely transition. I help you manage the complex grief, stress, and identity shifts that come with taking on a caregiver role.
  • Adult Children & Parent Dynamics: If you have grown but your family’s old patterns have not, it can cause constant tension. I help you set clear boundaries and improve communication, even when family dynamics feel stuck.

Lifestage & Identity Transitions

  • Young Adulthood & Independence: Stepping into full adulthood and navigating new levels of independence is a significant adjustment. I help you manage the uncertainty of “finding your way” while building a stable foundation for your future.
  • Mid-Life Realignment: Mid-life often feels like a paradox: you have built the life you were “supposed” to want, yet you feel restless or disconnected. I help you peel back the layers of outdated expectations to ensure your future is built on who you are today.
  • Older Adulthood & Vitality: Aging brings new perspectives and unique challenges. I provide a space to help you find purpose, maintain mental well-being, and navigate the changes that come with this stage of life.
  • Relocation & Geographic Shifts: Moving to a new city means losing your routine and support system. I help you process the impact of these changes and guide you in rebuilding a sense of belonging in your new environment.
