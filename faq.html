Life Transition Counseling and Therapy

What is the difference between counseling and therapy?

People often use “counseling” and “therapy” to mean the same thing, and there is a lot of overlap. Both provide a supportive space to work with a licensed professional on your mental and emotional well-being.

Counseling may be a good fit when you’re working through a specific life change—such as a career transition, relationship or family shift, or a period of high stress—and want support, perspective, and practical tools to move forward.

Therapy may be a better fit when you want to explore longer-standing patterns, past experiences, or concerns such as anxiety, depression, or trauma.

In my practice, I offer both. You don’t have to know which one you need before we begin. We’ll start with where you are and work together to figure out what kind of support will be most helpful for you.

How are sessions conducted?

I offer sessions in three formats: in person at my Cambridge and Brookline offices, online by secure video, and walk-and-talk sessions outdoors. Most clients choose one format and stay with it, but we can also mix formats — for example, meeting in person when your schedule allows and online when it does not.

In-office sessions are held at my Harvard Square office in Cambridge or my Beacon Street office in Brookline, by appointment only. See both office addresses and directions below.

Online sessions are held via a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform. This format is designed to provide you with consistent, flexible support that fits into your life while ensuring we maintain a high standard of clinical care. Online sessions are available to clients physically located in Massachusetts, Connecticut, Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, and Washington, DC.

To help us create a space where you can truly focus and feel at ease during an online session:

  • Please ensure you are in a private, quiet area where you feel comfortable speaking openly and know you won’t be overheard.
  • For your safety and to ensure you can give our sessions your full attention, I cannot conduct sessions while you are driving or operating any form of transportation.

Not sure which format fits? We can talk it through during a free 15-minute consultation, or you can email me at info@alicekimlicsw.com.

Because I am licensed in specific states, I can only provide therapy when you are physically located in a state where I hold a license at the time of our session. State licensing requirements are in place to ensure that therapists are practicing within the laws and professional standards of the state where the client is located.

What are your fees and do you accept insurance?

I am a private-pay therapist and do not bill insurance companies directly. My fee is $250 per session, which allows us to focus on your individual needs, goals, and care without the limitations or requirements that can come with insurance.

If you meet the criteria for a clinical mental health diagnosis, I can provide you with a superbill — a specialized invoice. If you have a PPO plan that includes out-of-network mental health benefits, you may be eligible for reimbursement for some of the cost of your sessions. Superbill receipts may also be used for reimbursement through an FSA or HSA depending on your plan.

If you do not qualify for a mental health diagnosis, I cannot provide a superbill. Please note that I do not communicate or coordinate with insurance providers on your behalf. Any reimbursement you receive should be considered a bonus rather than a guarantee for working together.

If you choose to submit a superbill, please keep in mind:

  • A mental health diagnosis is required
  • Insurance may request access to your treatment records
  • Reimbursement isn’t guaranteed

Before starting, it can be helpful to call your insurance provider and ask:

  • Do I have out-of-network mental health benefits?
  • What percentage do they cover?
  • What’s my deductible, and how much have I met?
  • How do I submit claims?
  • What are the rates for CPT codes 90791 ( initial evaluation) and 90834 (45 minutes)?
  • Is pre-authorization needed?

For more info, visit Zencare’s guide to out-of-network benefits.

Good Faith Estimate: By law, if you choose not to use your health insurance and opt to pay for therapy services out of pocket, you are entitled to a Good Faith Estimate of medical costs under the No Surprises Act. Click here for more information.

Why choose private pay instead of using insurance? What are the benefits?

Choosing private pay can seem costly and an extra step when you are already trying to find the right care provider. But for many clients, paying out-of-pocket offers greater flexibility, choice, and privacy in their care.

Benefits include:

  • Choose the Right Match and Expertise: You can choose a therapist based on their experience, expertise, and whether they feel like the right fit for you, rather than being limited to therapists who participate with your insurance plan.
  • Greater Flexibility in Your Care: We can work together to determine what type of support is most appropriate for you and how often we meet, without needing to meet your insurance company’s requirements for coverage or ongoing treatment.
  • Greater Privacy: When you use private pay and do not submit claims to insurance, you do not need to provide an insurance company with a mental health diagnosis or other treatment information for reimbursement. Your care remains between you and your therapist.
  • Simple and Transparent: The fee is clear and consistent, without having to navigate insurance deductibles, copays, or changing coverage. You know what your session will cost from the start.

Note on Insurance: Many insurance plans offer out-of-network mental health benefits. If you choose to submit sessions for potential reimbursement, your insurance company may require a diagnosis and information to determine medical necessity. If you meet the criteria for a mental health diagnosis, I can provide a superbill (a detailed receipt) that you can submit to your insurance company. Reimbursement is determined by your individual plan, and I recommend contacting your insurance company to understand your out-of-network benefits.

What is your cancellation policy?

When we schedule a session, that time is set aside exclusively for you. This is a mutual commitment that honors both our time and the important work we’re doing together. I maintain a limited number of clients to ensure you get the quality care you deserve. Providing at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations gives me the opportunity to offer that time to another client who may be waiting for support.

If you cannot make your appointment for any reason, please make sure to cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before our scheduled meeting. If you cancel less than 24 hours in advance or miss your appointment, you will be charged a $150 late cancellation/ no-show fee.
