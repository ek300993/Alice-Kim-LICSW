**28. The menu button now appears on tablets *(a change you should be aware of)***
The new logo is slightly wider than the old one (a 5.85:1 ratio versus 5.30:1 —
292px wide at full height, against 265px before). The navigation bar was already
a tight fit, and the extra width tipped it over: between roughly 768px and 950px
the logo was being crushed to 219px and rendered touching the "Home" link.

The nav genuinely could not hold the logo, five links and the consultation button
side by side at those widths, so the breakpoint where it collapses to the
hamburger menu button was moved from 767px up to 991px. **Practical effect: iPads
and similar tablets in portrait now get the menu button instead of the full link
bar.** Phones and desktops are unchanged.

Measured across 320px → 1600px, the logo is now never distorted, nothing
overlaps, and there is no horizontal overflow. If you would rather keep the full
link bar on tablets, the alternative is shortening the "Schedule a Free 15 Minute
Consultation" button label — it is the widest item in the bar.
